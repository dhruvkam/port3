Beliefs I build by

With receipts
The best interfaces disappear
Proof: Cut Walmart's finance dashboard from 12 metrics to 3. Time-to-insight improved 3x. Users stopped "using" the dashboard—they just got answers.
Constraints are gifts, not obstacles
Proof: At Sandbox AQ, shipped a quantum security tool in 2 months by saying "no" to 80% of requested features. The constraint forced clarity.
Ship to learn, not to impress
Proof: Cuemath's adaptive engine started as a spreadsheet prototype. Users testing the ugly version taught us more than 3 months of design reviews.
Second-order effects matter more than first
Proof: Gift Finder's first-order metric was conversion. Second-order: reduced returns by 20% because people bought things recipients actually wanted.
Watch behavior, ignore opinions
Proof: Users said they wanted more dashboard filters. Shadowing showed they used 2 of 15. We removed 13 filters. Complaints: zero. Usage: up 40%.
