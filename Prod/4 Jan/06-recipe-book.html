Ambiguity to Shippable Plan

For when you're handed a vague problem and expected to deliver something real.

Ingredients
  • Access to 5-8 stakeholders with different perspectives
  • One clear constraint (time, budget, or scope)
  • Whiteboard or digital equivalent
  • Willingness to say "no" to good ideas
Steps
  1. Interview stakeholders separately. Ask: "What does success look like in 6 months?"
  2. Map contradictions. Where do people disagree? That's where the real problem lives.
  3. Propose 3 directions. Force a choice by showing trade-offs.
  4. Prototype the riskiest assumption first. Build the smallest thing that tests it.
  5. Ship something in week 2. Feedback on real things beats opinions on ideas.
What you deliver A one-page plan with: problem statement, success metrics, phase 1 scope, and what you're explicitly not doing.

The Quick Validation Sprint

Test an idea before committing engineering resources.

Ingredients
  • A hypothesis worth testing
  • Access to 10+ potential users
  • Figma (or paper and markers)
  • 5 hours of focused time
Steps
  1. Write down your riskiest assumption in one sentence
  2. Design the smallest test that proves or disproves it
  3. Build a low-fi prototype (can be paper)
  4. Run 5 user sessions in 2 days
  5. Document: What surprised you? What would you change?
What you deliver A one-pager: hypothesis, test method, results, recommendation (kill/pivot/proceed).
📊
Typical time 3-4 weeks

Dashboard That Actually Gets Used

Build a decision-enabling interface, not a data graveyard.

Ingredients
  • Clear understanding of 3 key decisions users make
  • Access to decision-makers for shadowing
  • Data pipeline (existing or new)
  • Permission to remove features
Steps
  1. Shadow 5 users. Watch what they actually do, not what they say they do.
  2. List every decision. Then cut to the top 3.
  3. Design for those 3 decisions only. Everything else is hidden or removed.
  4. Test: Can a new user make a decision in under 30 seconds?
  5. Iterate based on time-to-decision, not "likes"
What you deliver A dashboard that loads fast, answers questions immediately, and requires zero training.
