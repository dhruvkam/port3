Tell me about a project where you changed your mind
D

Great question. The FDH GenAI Assistant project completely changed how I think about dashboards.

What I believed: More data = better decisions
What I learned: Less data, better presented = faster decisions

We started by building a comprehensive dashboard with 12 metrics. Usage was terrible. Executives would open it, scroll around, and leave without acting.

The breakthrough came when we watched users. They weren't "exploring data"—they had a specific question and wanted an answer. So we replaced the dashboard with a conversational interface.

Result: 3x faster time-to-insight. Less UI, more impact.
